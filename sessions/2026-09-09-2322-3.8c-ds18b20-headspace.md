## Task 1: Phase 3.8c on silicon — the 1-Wire driver had never talked to a DS18B20

**Completed:**
- **The bench sitting for issue #94.** Checks 1, 2 and 3b pass, plus DEC-010's
  never-measured downlink direction. Checks 3 (below zero) and 4 (sleep current) still need a
  freezer and a meter.
- **Two firmware defects, neither findable from a host test.** The DS18B20 path had never
  once worked on any board, with 22 green tests over it the whole time.
  - **Bit timing.** `pinMode()`/`digitalWrite()` cost ~14 µs each on this chip. A write-1
    budgeted at 6 µs held the line low **35.7 µs against a 15 µs limit**, so every 1 bit went
    out as a 0 and no command was ever understood. The 500 µs reset swallowed it, so the bus
    reported a healthy presence pulse and returned nine bytes of `0xFF`.
    `esp32/onewire_bus.cpp` now drives the slots by register write: **6.9 µs**.
  - **The part lies about conversion-complete.** Answers read slots with 1 while still
    converting. Proven by waiting 800 ms blind and getting a real temperature where polling
    got the +85 °C power-on value. `core/ds18b20.cpp` floors the wait at the part's real
    conversion time, read from the config byte *before* converting (`dsConversionMs()`).
- **Removed the heal's verify — DEC-016 supersedes DEC-015.** It needed `Ve` to fall below the
  part's reset threshold in 50 ms; measured, it does not collapse until **between 50 and
  100 ms**. So it never reset the part, read back the scratchpad it had just written, and
  reported success unconditionally. `verifyDischargeMs` and `healSucceededLastRead()` gone; a
  heal wake now costs one rail cycle instead of two.
- **`src/ds18probe/`** — new committed bench instrument beside `hw13` and `park`. Three
  controls plus a stage-by-stage walk of `read()`. It is the only thing that can measure any
  of the above.
- **The fake was more honest than the hardware**, which is why the bug shipped. Gained
  `lieAboutConversionStatus()`, and now holds the +85 °C power-on value until a conversion
  actually completes. `ignorePowerCycles()` removed with the verify.
- `main.cpp` heal-flag message corrected — it read *"could not write the probe's EEPROM"* on a
  node that had healed perfectly, left over from a design where the flag was set on failure
  rather than on attempt. `kFwVersion` `0x0109` → `0x010D`.
- `HARDWARE_BUILD_PLAN.md` §8 bench sitting updated with results, and the board-identity
  warning strengthened: **a firmware banner identifies the image, not the board.**

**Code review:** 3 findings, all confirmed and fixed in `f697cb7`. `begin()` had silently
become a *required* precondition rather than a safe early call — the old `driveLow()` set
direction and level together so the open-drain guarantee re-proved itself every call, and the
register version does not. Plus a redundant `bus_.reset()` at the top of `read()`, and a
duplicated build-plan paragraph that was also carrying two stale claims.

**Verified on hardware:** `fault_mask 0`, both channels in one packet, 27.0 °C on channel 4
and ~2251 mm on channel 8, downlink heard 7/7 at ~1996 ms into a 250 ms window. Probe healed
`0x7F` → `0x1F`. 207/207 native tests, both field builds compile, all doc gates clean.
