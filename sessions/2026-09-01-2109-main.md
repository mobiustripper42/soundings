### ⚠ Two defects in the issue's acceptance criteria

**1. `rtc_gpio_hold_en()` does not work on GPIO36.** The AC says to hold Vext across deep
sleep with it. The ESP32-S3 has two separate pin-hold mechanisms, one for RTC pins and one
for ordinary digital pins, and GPIO36 is the latter —
`~/.platformio/packages/framework-arduinoespressif32/tools/sdk/esp32s3/include/soc/esp32s3/include/soc/soc_caps.h:199`
sets `SOC_RTCIO_PIN_COUNT 22`, so RTC IO is GPIO0–21 only. The RTC call returns an error
and the pin goes unheld — which looks fine and is invisible until someone measures sleep
current. **Correct call: `gpio_hold_en(GPIO_NUM_36)` + `gpio_deep_sleep_hold_en()`**;
`.../driver/include/driver/gpio.h:377` states that digital-pad hold does not survive deep
sleep unless the second one is also called.

**2. "no new bit spent" is true, but a new sensor TYPE ID is spent.**
`firmware/src/core/nodemanifest.h:30-31` has only `kSensorTypeNone = 0` and
`kSensorTypeDistance = 1`. The DS18B20 needs `kSensorTypeDsTemp = 2` — a manifest-level id,
not a channel bit. DEC-007 is untouched by this; its "zero new bits" claim still holds.

### ⛔ The blocker — pins are unassigned, and this is the first thing to resolve

**No GPIO has ever been chosen for the A02YYUW's UART RX or the DS18B20's DQ line.**
Grepped `docs/`, `contracts/`, `firmware/src/`: neither appears anywhere. This is a real
gap, not a lookup failure.

Known-committed pins on the Wireless Stick Lite V3, from `firmware/src/esp32/sx1262_radio.h:46-49`
and `docs/CHAT_HANDOFF.md:430`: GPIO8 NSS, GPIO14 DIO1, GPIO12 RST, GPIO13 BUSY (plus the
SX1262 SPI bus), GPIO36 Vext Ctrl, GPIO37 ADC_Ctrl, GPIO1 VBAT sense, GPIO35 LED, GPIO43/44
CP2102 USB-serial.

**Selection criteria, in the order they bite:**

1. **Already spoken for** — the list above.
2. **Strapping pins.** Read at reset to choose boot mode. A pull-up holding one at the wrong
   level means the board does not boot, and it presents as a dead board rather than a wiring
   mistake. ⚠ **The DS18B20's 4.7 kΩ pull-up is the real exposure here, not the UART.**
3. **USB / JTAG pins** — reusing them costs the debug path.
4. **Flash / PSRAM pins** — usually not brought out, fatal if used.
5. **NOT a constraint, and worth stating because the instinct carries over wrongly:** every
   ESP32-S3 GPIO is bidirectional (the classic ESP32's input-only 34–39 rule does not
   apply), and the S3's GPIO matrix routes a UART to *any* pin. You are not picking "the
   UART pins" — you are picking any two free pins.

Once 1–4 are satisfied the deciding criterion is **physical**: two pins adjacent on the
header, on the side the gland enters, so the terminal-block run is short and legible.

⚠ **What is NOT established, and must not be guessed:** which GPIOs Heltec actually brings
out on the WSL V3's 2×20 header, and the S3's exact strapping-pin set. The board answer is
in the Rev1.1 datasheet already linked at `docs/CHAT_HANDOFF.md:430`. The Arduino variant
header at
`~/.platformio/packages/framework-arduinoespressif32/variants/heltec_wireless_stick_lite_v3/pins_arduino.h`
is **not** a reliable source for this — its `SCK = 36` / `MOSI = 35` collide with that same
file's `Vext = 36` and `LED = 35`, and none of its SPI values match the pins the radio
driver actually uses.

### Decisions the task must make

| Decision | Recommendation | Why |
|---|---|---|
| DS18B20 conversion resolution | **9-bit — operator agreed 2026-09-01** | 12-bit is 750 ms of held-awake time against §6's budget, where the ESP32 awake at ~40 mA is already 5× the sensor's cost (`docs/HARDWARE_BUILD_PLAN.md:611`) and the margin is ~1.48×. 9-bit is 93.75 ms and 0.5 °C steps — 1.76 mm of residual at a 2 m path against the 14 cm being corrected. The raw register stays 1/16 °C with low bits zero, so `derive.py:147` needs no change. |
| The 1-Wire seam | **`IOneWireBus` at byte level** — `reset()` returning presence, `writeByte()`, `readByte()` | Mirrors `IByteSource` exactly. Bit-banged 1-Wire timing is microseconds and cannot be host-tested; the scratchpad decode, CRC-8, POR-sentinel rejection and conversion-wait state machine all can. Driver in `core/`, timing in `esp32/`. |
| Dependency | **Hand-roll first; operator will take a library if it fights us** | `DallasTemperature` blocks in `delay()` (against the non-blocking convention) and hands back floats where we want raw i16. `OneWire` alone still leaves the scratchpad sequence to write. Fallback if hand-rolling gets difficult: pin `paulstoffregen/OneWire`. |
| Rail sharing | **Each driver does its own on/off — two rail cycles per wake** | Simpler than ref-counting, and it keeps the free-running A02YYUW off the rail while the DS18B20 converts. |

**A decision record is owed for the 9-bit choice.**
`grep -rli "ds18b20\|1-wire\|onewire\|resolution" docs/decisions/` returned DEC-006 and
DEC-007. DEC-007 settles the sensor's presence and the gateway-side correction and says
nothing about on-node resolution or seam shape — so this is a **new id, not an amendment**.

**Fault cases the DS18B20 driver must catch:** no presence pulse; scratchpad CRC-8 mismatch;

⚠ The band check is the **sensor's** range only — tank-plausibility stays gateway-side, the
same rule `firmware/src/core/a02yyuw.h:84` already states for distance.
