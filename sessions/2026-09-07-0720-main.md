**Code review:** 2 findings. **One real and load-bearing, fixed in `ff721a2`.** The heal's
verify cycles the rail and re-reads the config byte — but `off()`/`on()` are two GPIO writes
and `Ve` may never collapse, so a refusing part could report success. Under the original
"mark the flag only on failure" rule that left the flag clear, and every genuine wake from
deep sleep rewrote the part: ~35,000 writes/year against a 50,000-write rating, through the
mechanism built to prevent it. Fixed by marking the attempt **before** the write, so the
bound no longer depends on the verify being truthful. The fake could not have caught it —
it treated every off/on pair as a real power cycle — so `ignorePowerCycles()` was added and
the fix was confirmed by reverting it and watching two tests fail. Second finding (no settle
window before the first `reset()`) carried to the bench, where it fails loudly rather than
silently.
